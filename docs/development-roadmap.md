# Development roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (implemented now)

Monorepo structure, service scaffolds, database schema, architecture/security documentation, design tokens, and contract tool configuration.

## Phase 2 — Cinema operations

Admin authentication; movie, cinema, auditorium, seat-template, showtime, and price management.

## Phase 3 — Customer booking

Movie discovery, seat-map availability, short-lived holds, price review, and order lifecycle.

## Phase 4 — NFT ticket contract

ERC-721 implementation, access-control roles, mint uniqueness, non-transferability, check-in guard, Foundry tests, and Polygon Amoy deployment.

## Phase 5 — Web3 integration

Wallet connection, payment/mint confirmation tracking, IPFS metadata creation, transaction history, and My Tickets.

## Phase 6 — Gate operations

Signed QR generation, scanner experience, on-chain ticket verification, one-time redemption, and audit reports.

## Phase 7 — Quality and presentation

End-to-end tests, security review, demo data, deployment documentation, and university-project presentation material.
